Full Methodology in About Section
Scoring Physics

Evaluates van der Waals dispersion (Gauss 1/2), steric repulsion, hydrophobic contact, directional H-bonds, and torsional loss.

Search Algorithm

100% blind multi-start search with quaternion rotations, adaptive soft-core clash dampening, and Iterated Local Search (ILS).

Zero-Disk Policy

100% volatile memory lifecycle. Structures and poses reside in RAM buffers and are never stored to disk or database tables.

Academic Deliverables

1-click export of merged Complex (.PDB), AutoDock Multi-Model Poses (.PDBQT), and Publication Summary Table (.CSV).

Pharma Zero-Storage Invariant

Strict Computational Chemistry Privacy

100% Volatile RAM Execution: When you perform molecular docking on iLoveChem, your macromolecular receptor PDB and ligand chemical structures are parsed directly in volatile server memory.

Zero Disk or Database Persistence: We do not log, persist, or store your target receptors, SMILES strings, or docked conformations on server disks or databases. Once the HTTP response finishes, all memory buffers are garbage-collected.

No External Binary Wrappers: Docking calculations are carried out entirely in-process using our vectorized empirical scoring engine, eliminating security vulnerabilities associated with running untrusted subprocess binaries.

ChemDock Scoring Algorithm

AutoDock Empirical Free Energy Formulation

ChemDock implements the empirical free energy of binding scoring function (Trott & Olson, 2010):

ΔG = c_inter Σ (f_gauss1 + f_gauss2 + f_repulsion + f_hydrophobic + f_hbond) / (1 + 0.05846 · N_rot)
  • Steric Attractions (Gauss 1 & Gauss 2): Short and intermediate range dispersion and attractive van der Waals interactions.
  • Steric Repulsion: Quadratic penalties for atomic inter-penetration and clash prevention.
  • Hydrophobic Interactions: Energetic bonus for pairs of non-polar carbon and halogen atoms in close proximity.
  • Directional Hydrogen Bonding: Linear ramp rewarding favorable hydrogen bond donor/acceptor contact pairs within 1.9 – 2.5 Å.
  • Torsional Entropy Penalty: Free energy penalty scaled by the number of active rotatable bonds (N_rot) in the ligand torsion tree.
