About iLoveChem & Platform Capabilities

                        
Designed & Developed by Shivam Rathore

                    

                

                
            


            

                
                

                    

                        
                        Universal In-Memory Chemistry Conversion Engine
                    

                    

                        iLoveChem bridges the gap between 2D chemical drawing tools, molecular docking platforms, and high-performance quantum chemistry DFT solvers. Built from the ground up for zero-install, instant, and secure chemical informatics.
                    

                


                
                

                    
Core Scientific Capabilities

                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
                                15+ Chemical File Formats
                            

                            

                                Instant bi-directional conversion between PDB, AutoDock PDBQT, SDF, MOL2, CIF crystals, XYZ clusters, ChemDraw CDXML, Gaussian / ORCA logs and input decks (.com, .gjf, .inp).
                            

                        


                        

                            

                                
                                Valence & Spin Multiplicity Guard
                            

                            

                                Automatically audits total valence electrons to assign accurate spin states (singlet vs open-shell doublet / triplet), preventing SCF convergence failures in ORCA and Gaussian.
                            

                        


                        

                            

                                
                                Real-Time MedChem Ro5 Radar
                            

                            

                                Computes Lipinski Rule of 5 descriptors on the fly: Molecular Weight (MW), Wildman-Crippen LogP, H-Bond Donors (HBD), H-Bond Acceptors (HBA), TPSA (Å²), and Rotatable Bonds.
                            

                        


                        

                            

                                
                                Reaction Scheme ZIP Unbundler
                            

                            

                                Intelligently filters reaction arrows, conditions, and written text from ChemDraw CDXML sheets, unbundling multiple reaction components into 3D files packaged in a single ZIP.

Zero-Storage & RAM Policy

                        
Confidentiality Guarantee

                    

                

                
            


            

                

                    

                        ✓ 100% Volatile RAM Execution
                    

                    

                        Chemical files and structures exist purely in temporary Python memory buffers and are permanently discarded upon conversion.
                    

                


                

                    

                        ✓ Zero Database Persistence
                    

                    

                        No database models, tables, or storage buckets store molecular graphs, SMILES strings, or coordinates.
                    

                


                

                    

                        ✓ Zero AI Training Data Collection
                    

                    

                        Your proprietary drug candidates and unpublished coordinates are NEVER logged, saved, or used for model training.
